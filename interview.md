## **Engineering Interviews @ Rilla**

This guide is meant to help set you up for success with the interview process here at Rilla.

**Overall Interview Process & What to Expect**
This is a walkthrough of the whole interview process and how to prepare for each.

**Initial Screen (30 min)**
 
This first interview is with one of our engineers. They will use this call to get to know you, learn more about your experience and motivations, and tell you more about Rilla.

**Technical Screen (1 hour)**
 
 
**Technical Screen: Coding Exercise**
This technical screen will be conducted with a member of the engineering team. It will be done on a CoderPad screen, which has an entire VS Code environment. It is built on top of an existing project. You can choose either Typescript or Python for the exercise. Usage of LLMs is prohibited in this exercise.
 
**Programming (55 min) - live coding exercise**
 
5 min q&a
This interview will be conducted through Zoom and CoderPad. Be prepared to share your screen on Zoom.

### Onsite
 
You will be building a project from scratch at our on-site and showing it to the interviewers at the end. This will be building it from the ground up, starting from 0 lines of code. This will emulate the kind of work we do here at Rilla as a software engineer.
The schedule for the day will be:
 
**Kickoff (30 min)** - An engineer will sit down with you and walk you through the prompt and the data you’ll be working with. This is to be a conversation to answer any questions you have and provide as much context as you’ll need. You’ll start brainstorming your approach here. Think about sitting in a room with a whiteboard, talking through what your solution will initially look like, and understanding the context.
 
**Building (4 hours)** - You start working. You can use any tool to accomplish this task. This is meant to feel like a real workday. Halfway through this, an engineer from Rilla will sit down with you for a 30-minute pair-programming session. They will ask you to walk through what you have built so far, the decisions you’ve made so far, and where you are headed. They will pair with you on something you are working on in that moment.
 
**Presentation (1 hour)** - You present what you built to the interviewers. Walk through the product, the architecture, and the decisions you made. The interviewers will be digging into many parts of your solution. We will also ask you how you will solve the same problem in a production setting on a larger scale, close to a systems design interview.
 
**Culture Interview (30 min)** - A conversation with our CTO to assess your approach, values, and how you align with Rilla’s fast-paced, ownership-driven engineering culture.
 
We will provide a laptop for you to use. The laptop will have accounts for popular coding tools like Claude Code, Codex, and Cursor, with limits on spending. You can bring your own laptop and tools if you prefer.

### CEO Screen (30+ min)
 
Next, you will move on to a screen with the CEO, Sebastian. Sebastian is not technical, so don’t expect this to be a technical call. He’ll be digging into your motivations and experience, and this will also be a chance for you to ask any questions you have about the company.

### References
 
The final step in the process is reference checks. All offers are contingent on these reference checks, and we do not move forward until these are complete. You will also have an opportunity to talk to one of our investors to learn more about Rilla from an outside perspective.
